WHAT V0.8 CAN AND CANNOT CLAIM

A PASS is a completeness/QA signal, not a grade guarantee.

Automated evidence

Project tasks, assignment/rubric criteria, V0.7 evidence coverage, stale-map detection, Dataset Preflight metadata and Feedback Memory signals.

Manual evidence

Your explicit confirmations for source-number tracing, visual review, narrative/recommendation support and final-file readiness.

Not proved

Instructor judgment, hidden grading criteria, actual SQL/database execution, workbook correctness, dashboard interactions, factual business correctness, or the grade you will receive.
